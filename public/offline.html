Ride Muni like a local

Your guide teaches you how to buy and use a Muni day pass. Guests buy their own fares. The day pass covers Muni buses, historic streetcars and light rail; cable cars and BART are separate.

Buy and activate your pass before boarding. Keep proof of payment ready and your phone charged. Let riders off first and keep doors and priority seats clear.
