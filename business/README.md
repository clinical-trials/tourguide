The September 15 full business plan models 12 Branch B departures at a proposed $149 ticket, four guests minimum and eight maximum, with the $3,000 starting-cash allowance still subject to quotes. At four, five and six guests per departure, operating surplus after owner operating labor is $2,771.23, $4,426.14 and $6,081.05. After initial setup cash and setup labor, the respective residuals are $781.23, $2,436.14 and $4,091.05 before income taxes. Mathematical full-cost break-even is 43 guests; running all 12 dates at the four-person minimum requires 48. These are scenarios, not measured attendance or profit.
