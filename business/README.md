The PDF manuals and ZIP bundles are dated snapshots. For subsequent website changes, use the repository's [OPERATIONS.md](../OPERATIONS.md), [TAXES.md](../TAXES.md) and source code. The latest booking update preserves selections when returning from checkout and explicitly lists coffee, food and drinks as paid separately in the booking review.

The pricing addendum compares official Alcatraz adult fares ($47.95 day, $59.65 night, $104.65 Behind the Scenes) with our existing cost model. At the modeled return take-up and costs, $50, $75 and $100 tour tickets require 220, 154 and 118 guests/month respectively to break even. These are planning scenarios; the website and workbook retain $195 until a new selling price is selected. The addendum is a separate download and is not included in the earlier ZIP bundles.

The banner QR points to the intended domain, `aisftour.com`; domain connection and the QR destination must be verified before printing for public use. The [deployed review website](https://ai-sf-tour.yoyoyoyoyoyoyoyoyoyoyo.chatgpt.site/) currently requires the owner's signed-in account, and ticket sales remain closed.
