# AI SF Tour: internal Muni teaching notes

For guides delivering the lesson in person. Fare guidance checked September 8, 2026.

Public promise: “We teach you how to enjoy the city the way the locals do.” The website describes that experience without publishing this tutorial. Guests purchase their own Muni passes and fares, including for the optional guided return. Help them choose an option appropriate to their existing fare and eligibility.

## Teach during the tour

1. **Know the coverage.** The Muni-Only Day Pass covers buses, historic streetcars and Muni Metro light rail through 11:59 p.m. on the day of use. It is not a 24-hour ticket. Cable cars, BART and Caltrain need separate fares. [SFMTA day pass](https://www.sfmta.com/fares/day-pass).
2. **Use the right method.** If the pass is on Clipper, tap the same card or digital Clipper card at each boarding or Metro entry. If it is in MuniMobile, activate it before boarding or entering faregates and show the active animated ticket when asked; it does not work on Clipper readers. A farebox paper pass is kept and shown. Ask the station agent for the appropriate gate when using a pass that cannot be tapped. [Clipper pass instructions](https://www.clippercard.com/ClipperWeb/transit-agency-tips), [MuniMobile instructions](https://www.sfmta.com/munimobile-frequently-asked-questions).
3. **Ride with confidence.** Match the line and destination to your trip. Let people exit first. A valid pass permits any-door boarding; cash purchases require the front door. Use the stop cord or button where provided. Keep proof of payment available throughout the journey, and keep your phone charged if it holds your fare. Muni does not require tapping out. [How to ride](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/how-ride-muni-quick-start-guide), [Proof of payment](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/fares/proof-payment).
4. **Keep exploring afterward.** Before the group separates, have the guide help you find a route, direction and stop for your next destination. You can continue using your pass until its expiry.

**Paper purchase practice:** tell the operator you want a day pass before putting exact cash into the farebox. Check the printed expiry and retain the pass. The current published day-pass price is $5.70, paid by the guest when a pass is needed. [Day-pass purchase instructions](https://www.sfmta.com/fares/day-pass).

**Guests aged 16–18:** regular Muni is free, with no day-pass purchase required. SFMTA encourages carrying ID for age verification. The guided-tour ticket is a separate purchase. [Youth fares](https://www.sfmta.com/fares/free-muni-all-youth-18-years-and-younger).

**App changes:** SFMTA plans to end MuniMobile day-pass sales January 3, 2027. Its single-ride sales already ended September 1, 2026. The guide should recheck the official page before each departure and teach the fare method actually being used. [MuniMobile transition](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/fares/munimobile).

## Guide delivery: demonstrate, practice, check

- **Before guests arrive:** prepare to help guests choose and purchase their own fare. Confirm they can keep using it after the tour. Know a workable option for guests without a smartphone or usable app account. Ask about existing passes and youth eligibility before recommending any purchase. Guest passes are not supplied or funded by the tour.
- **During the welcome:** allow roughly five minutes within the welcome and first transit segment. Point out which vehicles are covered, demonstrate the group's fare method, and have each guest locate their card, active ticket or paper pass. Treat this duration as a rehearsal allowance, not a tested timetable.
- **Before boarding or crossing the faregate:** check that each guest has the correct valid fare or youth eligibility. Have guests perform their own tap or activation while the guide assists. Resolve a rejected card or inactive ticket before entering the paid area. Keep the group out of the boarding path while explaining.
- **On the first transfer:** let a guest identify the line and direction, then confirm it. Ask whether their pass should be tapped or shown on the next ride. Demonstrate station entry where the route uses Metro.
- **At the finish:** ask each guest to explain three things: which vehicles their pass covers, what they do at the next boarding, and when it expires. Help them plan one onward journey and make sure their fare remains accessible when the group separates.

Build this into the timed A/B trials and the eventual Branch C trial. Keep the advertised A/B departure and finish times; shorten introductory storytelling if needed and measure the actual teaching time. The lesson does not change Branch C's unconfirmed fare inclusions or its weather requirement.
