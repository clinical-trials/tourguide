## Money to plan for

Startup estimate including 15% contingency: **$4,635**. With two months' committed costs including owner pay, the modeled funding requirement is **$28,429**. These are planning assumptions, not supplier quotes or a revenue forecast.

At eight places per departure, two departures and six days each week, the workbook models 416 monthly places. Validate demand for $195 before relying on occupancy scenarios. The full operating schedule totals about **267 owner hours per month** in the workbook. Consider a backup guide and a small pilot before committing to that schedule.

At the assumed $40 hourly guide rate, a 45-minute return costs $30 in labor. One $40 buyer covers that marginal labor; the modeled contribution after card fees and the reserve is $37.34 per buyer. At unchanged 25% return take-up, the base-case return contributes about $382 monthly after staffing every departure. Take-up at the higher price has not been tested. Review the workbook before promising the service on every departure.

## Decisions needed to open sales

1. Confirm whether Branch A and Branch B are different tours and whether $195 is per branch. Confirm fares, capacity, minimum group, online cutoff, cancellation terms and tax treatment.
2. Complete timed route trials with a real group. Set exact meeting/finish points, accessible alternatives, restroom and cafe arrangements. Check the applicable business, insurance and location permissions.
3. Confirm control of **aisftour.com**, connect its DNS and choose the public launch date. The sign's QR already points there; test it on a phone after the domain works.
4. Connect the operator's Stripe account securely. Configure a reachable signed webhook and run test payments, cancellations, refunds and capacity checks before enabling live sales. Do not send secret keys in chat.
5. Approve the public contact details, booking terms and final route copy. Then enable public access and ticket sales together.

The website's private access is intentional during review. It will need a publicly reachable webhook when Stripe is tested or used. No domain was bought or changed; no accounts, messages, payments or sign orders were created on your behalf.
