| Mandatory booking, processing and operator charges | Include in every advertised tour price. California’s pricing rule allows exclusion of taxes or fees imposed by government **on the transaction**, not a general surcharge to recover business expenses. An optional return may be separately priced. [California Attorney General FAQ](https://www.oag.ca.gov/hiddenfees) |
| California sales tax | Regulation 1501 distinguishes service transactions from sales of tangible property. Our inference is that this standalone guide service, including the guided return, is generally outside sales tax. CDTFA’s destination-management guide also discusses nontaxable tour/sightseeing services, but is not an account-specific ruling for this operator. Do not automatically apply SF’s retail sales-tax rate. Confirm the exact offer with CDTFA or the operator’s tax adviser and retain the conclusion. [Regulation 1501](https://cdtfa.ca.gov/lawguides/vol1/sutr/1501.html), [CDTFA travel-service guidance](https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/industry/destination-management-companies/) |
| SF business registration | The published 2026–27 schedule lists **$55 plus a $4 state fee** for its lowest receipts band, $0–$100,000. This is a business registration cost, not a per-ticket tax or the complete cost of launching. The applicable band and new-business amount depend on the registration facts. [SF registration and fee schedule](https://www.sftreasurer.org/business/register-business) |
| SF guide permit and annual license | Police Code §1295 requires a written Police permit and advance licensing for paid guiding, plus an identifying guide badge. Current application, annual license and any fingerprint charges need a quote from SFPD. The code’s old base dollar amounts should not be treated as current invoices; certified schedules include adjustments. These are operator costs. [Guide ordinance](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_police/0-0-0-5570), [SFPD application process](https://www.sanfranciscopolice.org/get-service/permits) |

SF’s reform guidance says most businesses with SF gross receipts of $5 million or less qualify for small-business relief while still registering annually. [SF business tax reform](https://www.sftreasurer.org/proposition-m-2024-business-tax-reform) |

## Fisherman’s Wharf meeting point and advertising stand

“At Fisherman’s Wharf” is not precise enough to verify site permission or its cost. Record the exact address/map pin, which side of the sidewalk, maximum group size (eight guests plus guide), check-in duration, whether tickets will be sold on site, and the proposed sign dimensions and placement.

The Port issues permits for sidewalk/street encroachments and events on its property. Its **Tenant Sign Guidelines**, governing entities leasing or licensing Port property, prohibit portable signs such as sandwich boards except for specified parking uses. Temporary event signage has a separate review process. Those tenant rules do not, by themselves, determine every handheld sign or every non-Port sidewalk location. The proposed wheeled/portable banner is not approved merely because it is movable. Obtain the responsible land manager’s determination before placing or selling beside it. [Port services](https://www.sfport.com/services), [Port Tenant Sign Guidelines, pp. 6–7](https://www.sfport.com/sites/default/files/2021-08/Sign%20Guidelines.pdf).

If the selected area is in San Francisco Maritime National Historical Park, including an Aquatic Park location, use the park’s own permissions process; a City guide license does not establish NPS authorization. Branch C’s Ocean Beach activity also needs the applicable Golden Gate National Recreation Area commercial-use authorization. The published GGNRA guided-service schedule lists a $350 annual application charge and $300 per guide placard. These are operator expenses, not an automatic A/B ticket surcharge. [Maritime park permissions](https://www.nps.gov/safr/planyourvisit/permitsandreservations.htm), [GGNRA commercial-use authorization](https://www.nps.gov/goga/learn/management/cua.htm).
