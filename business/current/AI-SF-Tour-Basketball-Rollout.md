Prioritize October 10 and October 23, subject to readiness and the four-paying-guest minimum/eight-guest maximum in the existing pilot proposal. October 6 is optional. These are initial candidates, not all twelve dates of the existing six-paid-week pilot. The pilot clock starts at the first paid operating week; choose additional confirmed home games after the first results.

Game attendance is not proof of tour demand. Keep pricing unchanged pending the separate price decision, and compare contribution after guide labor and referral costs rather than gross sales. Conditional Valkyries games contribute no committed revenue forecast.
