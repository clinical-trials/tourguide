## Rollout decisions and measurement

Prioritize October 10 and October 23 as **Warriors** candidates, subject to readiness and the four-paying-guest minimum/eight-guest maximum in the existing pilot proposal. October 6 is optional. Hold a flexible opportunity for a Valkyries home-game pilot within the October playoff window; choose its actual date only after official confirmation, and check guide capacity if both teams offer nearby dates. These are initial candidates, not all twelve dates of the existing six-paid-week pilot. The pilot clock starts at the first paid operating week; choose additional confirmed home games after the first results.

Track each team and game separately: qualified leads, paid guests, booking source, $25 hotel referrals per eligible completed full-price guest, acquisition cost, guide preparation/delivery hours, refunds, guest rating and arrival buffer achieved. Keep preseason and regular-season results separate. Require the timed rehearsal to preserve the full 60-minute buffer before releasing a game-day departure.

Game attendance is not proof of tour demand. Keep pricing unchanged pending the separate price decision, and compare contribution after guide labor and referral costs rather than gross sales. Conditional Valkyries games contribute no committed revenue forecast.
